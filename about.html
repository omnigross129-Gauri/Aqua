About us

Reliable Water & Wastewater
Treatment Solutions

Aqua J1 Technologies is a rapid growing and trusted name in the manufacturing, designing and erecting of water and wastewater Treatment Solutions across India. From our inception in 2011 we have been delivering reliable, efficient and sustainable treatment systems designed to meet the evolving needs of industrial, commercial establishments and infrastructure projects.

Our expertise spans a wide range of water treatment plants (WTP), Wastewater Treatment Plants (WWTP), and Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems, catering to diverse commercial and industrial applications. With a strong focus on engineering excellence and customer satisfaction, our multidisciplinary team is dedicated to deliver innovative solutions providing clean water for a cleaner future.
