Zico Alka

Zico Alka is a modern domestic water purifier built to combine strong purification performance with alkaline enhancement. It uses RO, UV, UF, sediment and carbon filtration stages with MTDS control to improve water safety and taste from a wide range of input sources. With 10-15 liters per hour purification capacity and 12-liter storage, the model is suited for families looking for dependable daily drinking water with a premium look.
