Elegance

Elegance is a premium domestic water purifier built for users who need both performance and modern design. It combines RO, UF, alkaline enhancement, and multi-stage carbon-sediment filtration to improve water quality across different input sources. The smart digital display and integrated TDS controller help users monitor and manage output water quality with ease. With a purification capacity of 12-15 liters per hour and 9-liter storage, Elegance is suitable for families and office spaces seeking consistent and safe drinking water.
